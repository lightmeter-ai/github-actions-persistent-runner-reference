# Support

This repository is an archived historical source release. Lightmeter does not
provide support for installing, adapting, operating, or debugging it.

In particular:

- no feature, compatibility, or future-release commitment exists;
- issues, pull requests, and Discussions are not accepted;
- no supported operating system, runner version, container runtime, or cloud
  provider is declared;
- the in-memory backend is a test oracle, not a production implementation; and
- no Lightmeter service depends on this repository.

You may fork and adapt the material under the Apache License 2.0. You are
responsible for reviewing the design against your own provider, host, privilege,
isolation, and threat model.
